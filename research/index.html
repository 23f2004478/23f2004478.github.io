Multi-Class Affect Detection in Educational Chatbots: Evidence from Imbalanced Dialogue Data

Accepted for publication in Springer Nature proceedings, Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies series (Scopus indexed) · FICTA 2026

Context and Problem

Educational dialogues contain severe class imbalance. Critical emotional states like confusion and frustration indicate when a learner is struggling, but they appear far less frequently than neutral or positive acknowledgments. Conventional classification algorithms optimize for overall accuracy, which allows them to score well while failing to detect the minority emotional states where pedagogical intervention is most needed.

Methodology

I paired RoBERTa, a pretrained language model, with SVMSMOTE, a method that creates extra examples of the rare emotion classes so the model sees enough of them. I judged the models by how many of the rare Confused cases they caught (recall), not by overall accuracy.

Key Findings

The proposed RoBERTa plus SVMSMOTE architecture achieved a 74.19% recall on the rare Confused emotion class, compared to less than 52% recall for baseline architectures without synthetic oversampling.

Venue

Primary Metric

Architecture

Adoption and Implications of Generative AI in Marketing: A Systematic Review and Research Agenda

Presented at the 10th Pan-IIM World Management Conference 2025 · IIM Ranchi

Context and Objective

Enterprise interest in generative AI for marketing automation has grown rapidly, but academic literature remained fragmented across practitioner whitepapers and narrow technical benchmarks. The review pulls together what published studies say about how companies use generative AI in marketing.

Review Protocol

Following the PRISMA review method, I reviewed 29 Scopus articles published from 2020 to 2025 across technology readiness, consumer adoption, and regulatory considerations.

Synthesis and Proposed Agenda

The review synthesised research priorities across algorithmic transparency in personalized marketing, consumer trust in synthetic media, workflow hand-offs between marketers and generative models, and data governance for automated campaigns.

Method
PRISMA Systematic Literature Review
Corpus

Focus
Generative AI in marketing
