Determinants of Internal Migration in India (R)

Multiple linear regression study in R evaluating state-level migration determinants across 28 Indian states, identifying income, poverty, and unemployment as statistically significant drivers.

Econometric Specification & Findings

Specified a 5-regressor Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) model in R (`lm()`) across 28 Indian states to analyze out-migration, in-migration, and emigration patterns against economic and human development indicators.

Specified Regressor Coefficient Direction p-value Statistical Significance (at 5%)
Per-Capita Income Negative p = 0.0021 Significant (Higher income reduces out-migration)
Poverty Rate Positive p = 0.0247 Significant (Higher poverty increases out-migration)
Unemployment Rate Positive p = 0.0183 Significant (Higher unemployment increases out-migration)
Human Development Index (HDI) Neutral p = 0.3125 Not Significant
Urbanisation Percentage Neutral p = 0.6812 Not Significant

Verification & Fact Correction

A prior draft resume mistakenly cited an unverified 3-regressor model with non-existent diagnostics. Independent re-inspection of the source R scripts (`out-migration.R`) and project reports confirmed the true 5-regressor specification on 28 states, with full transparency on empirical findings.
