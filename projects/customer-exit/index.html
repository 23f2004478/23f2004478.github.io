Customer exit prediction: 8-classifier benchmark on 90,000 rows

Financial institution churn. 8 classifiers benchmarked on validation F1. Tuned Random Forest: F1 0.6365, accuracy 0.8414. Accuracy flatters a skewed target; F1 is the honest number.

Period
T2 2026
Stack
Python, scikit-learn, LightGBM, XGBoost, RandomizedSearchCV, IterativeImputer
Metric
F1 (imbalanced binary classification)
Training rows
90,000

Context

Predicting customer exit (churn) at a financial institution. The target is imbalanced: most customers do not exit. Accuracy 0.8414 is the flattering number; F1 0.6365 is the honest one. Features include credit score, country, gender, age, tenure, account balance, product count, card ownership, activity flag, and estimated salary.

Approach

  • Missing values in credit_score, country, acc_balance, and prod_count imputed with mode (categorical) and IterativeImputer (numerical).
  • 8 classifiers benchmarked on validation F1.
  • Top 3 (Random Forest, LightGBM, XGBoost) tuned with RandomizedSearchCV against F1.

Results

  • Tuned Random Forest: F1 0.6365, accuracy 0.8414, precision 0.6181, recall 0.6561
  • Best CV F1: 0.6456
  • Tuned LightGBM: 0.6334 · Tuned XGBoost: 0.6309
  • XGBoost and LightGBM were GPU-accelerated

Figures from executed notebook (50 retained outputs); last checked 2026-10-03.

Limits

  • Accuracy 0.8414 flatters a skewed target. Lead with F1 0.6365.
  • Feature importance (Balance_to_Salary_Ratio as top predictor) is mentioned in presentation script but not output-verified in the notebook.
  • No Gini or other single-threshold metrics are claimed. F1 is the primary metric throughout.
