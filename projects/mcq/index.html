Smart MCQ solver: transformer ensemble with LoRA fine-tuning

Applied machine learning, including parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA) of two transformer models. MAP@3 0.74242 on Kaggle public leaderboard, 500 unseen questions.

Period
May 2026 to Jul 2026
Stack
Python, HuggingFace PEFT, DeBERTa-v3-base, RoBERTa-base, Weights and Biases
Metric
MAP@3
Evaluation
Kaggle public leaderboard, 500 unseen questions

Context

5-option MCQ ranking over 2,000 training questions. The objective is MAP@3: the mean average precision at 3, which rewards putting the correct answer in the top 3 ranked positions.

Four NLP architectures were benchmarked. DeBERTa-v3-base and RoBERTa-base produced the strongest individual scores; a weighted ensemble outperformed either alone.

Limits

  • The adapters are trained on course-provided data; no claims are made about generalization to other MCQ domains.
