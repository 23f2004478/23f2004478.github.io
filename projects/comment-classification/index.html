Comment classification: feature engineering and predictive modelling

F1-Macro from 0.6221 to 0.8293 on 39.6K held-out rows. 198K comments, 4 label classes, 100-trial Optuna study, 3-model blend.

Period
Jan 2026 to Mar 2026
Stack
Python, LightGBM, scikit-learn, Optuna, Nelder-Mead
Metric
F1-Macro (imbalanced classes)
Held-out set
39.6K rows

Context

198K comments across 4 label classes. The target distribution is imbalanced, so accuracy flatters the majority class. F1-Macro weights all classes equally and is the appropriate metric here.

What I did

  • Analyzed 198K comments across 4 label classes with F1-Macro as the primary metric.
  • Screened 11 tabular predictors over 198K rows with chi-square and Kruskal-Wallis tests, ranked by test statistic.
  • Engineered 10K+ word and character-level n-grams with behavioral signals to detect obfuscated and toxic patterns.
  • Ran a 100-trial Optuna study over 10 LightGBM hyperparameters, median-pruning 62 trials on 3-fold CV. Best CV F1: 0.807.
  • Built a 3-model blend (Logistic Regression, LightGBM, one additional), optimizing 4 per-class multipliers with Nelder-Mead.

Results

  • Baseline (Logistic Regression): F1-Macro 0.6221
  • LightGBM (Optuna tuned): F1-Macro 0.8202
  • 3-model blend (final): F1-Macro 0.8293 on 39.6K held-out rows
  • Gain over baseline: +0.2072

Figures from executed notebook; last checked 3 Oct 2026. No leaderboard rank shown.

Limits

  • Competition leaderboard rank is not shown; the rank figure in some earlier versions was unsourced.
  • Optuna tuning completed for LightGBM only; XGBoost studies have zero recorded values.
  • Nelder-Mead tunes 4 per-class multipliers on the blend, not per-model weights. Its gain is +0.0021, from 0.8293 to 0.8313. LightGBM alone scores 0.8202.
