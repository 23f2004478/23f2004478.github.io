Methodology & Engineering Decisions

1. Feature Engineering & Statistical Screening

Engineered over 10,000 word and character-level n-grams combined with behavioral text signals. Screened 11 tabular predictors across 198K rows using chi-square and Kruskal-Wallis statistical tests, ranking predictors on test statistics to eliminate noisy dimensions.

2. Hyperparameter Optimization

Conducted a 100-trial Optuna study optimizing 10 hyperparameters for LightGBM. Implemented median pruning on 3-fold cross-validation, pruning 62 unpromising trials early to focus compute on high-potential regions, reaching a best cross-validation score of 0.807 CV F1.

3. Ensemble Optimization

Combined baseline models into a 3-model blend. Using Nelder-Mead simplex optimization to tune 4 per-class probability decision thresholds, the ensemble achieved a 0.8293 F1-Macro score on the 39.6K held-out evaluation dataset, representing a +0.0091 gain over the tuned standalone LightGBM model.
