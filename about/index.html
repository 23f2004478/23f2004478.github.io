About Krishnendu Biswas

Systems builder focused on deterministic automation, machine learning engineering, and transparent, verifiable reporting.

Engineering Philosophy

I focus on turning messy, repetitive workflows into deterministic, verifiable systems. Whether architecting a 12-profile agent estate or tuning tree-based models on 90,000 imbalanced banking records, my approach is grounded in three principles:

  1. Deterministic First: Exact computation, regex parsing, and SQL queries always precede LLM token consumption. AI reasoning is reserved for genuinely ambiguous tasks.
  2. Honest Metrics: In class-imbalanced datasets, raw accuracy is deceptive. I evaluate models on F1-Macro, Precision-Recall balance, and cross-validation stability.
  3. Traceable Provenance: Every number, benchmark score, and architectural diagram is traceable to an on-disk source file and reproducible notebook.
