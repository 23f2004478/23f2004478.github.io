Who is Krishnendu Biswas?

Krishnendu Biswas is a 2nd-year MBA student at IIM Ranchi (class of 2027) and an AI agent builder who builds multi-agent platforms and runs them daily behind a human approval gate. He is pursuing the BS Degree in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras, where he completed the Diploma in Programming. He serves on the Corporate Relations and Placement Committee at IIM Ranchi and is an author of a research paper on dialogue affect detection accepted for publication in Springer Nature FICTA 2026 proceedings, a Scopus indexed series.
