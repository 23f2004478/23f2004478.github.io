Why you can trust the agents

Agents handle sensitive work. Layers of checks keep it from leaking or running unsupervised.

Agents do the work. Layers of checks keep it safe. When a layer fails, the next one catches it.

Defence in depth means no single control has to be perfect, because another layer is watching. A fail-safe is a backup that acts on its own if one layer misses something.

Who is Krishnendu Biswas?

Krishnendu Biswas is a 2nd-year MBA student at IIM Ranchi (class of 2027) and an AI agent builder who builds multi-agent platforms and runs them daily behind a human approval gate. He has studied the BS Degree in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras since May 2023, and completed the Diploma in Programming. He serves on the Corporate Relations and Placement Committee at IIM Ranchi and is an author of a research paper on dialogue affect detection accepted for publication in Springer Nature FICTA 2026 proceedings, a Scopus indexed series.
