Chapter 5 of 8

What the agents actually do

This is what happens between a voice note and a finished task. The risky steps stop and wait for me.

01 Input

Any-Device Ingest

I dictate on phone or Mac and send it on Discord or Telegram.

02 Router

Central Triage & Router

The router finds the owner and files a kanban task.

03 Agents

Domain agents

Specialists run all day on a server, each with its own memory.

04 Models

Model choice

Harder tasks go to stronger models. Plain scripts run first.

05 waits for me

The Human Gate

Pushes, emails, deletions and ledger writes wait for me.

06 Output

Delivery & Ledger Sync

Results land in Google Tasks, GitHub, the ledger or a chat note.
