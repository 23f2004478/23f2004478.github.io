Venture Capital Deal Analytics Dashboard in Power BI

Engineered a 50,000-row star schema dimensional model, authored 7 core DAX measures, and built a 3-page interactive executive dashboard with AI diagnostics.

Context IIM Ranchi DWDV Elective
Timeline Aug 2026
Certification Power BI Associate (PL-300)
Architecture Star Schema + DAX

The Reporting Challenge in Venture Capital

Venture capital investment committees manage complex multi-vintage portfolios across various financing stages (Seed, Series A, Series B, Growth) and diverse industry verticals. Monitoring capital deployment velocity, round valuations, and realized exit multiples typically requires consolidating messy spreadsheets across multiple fund managers.

The objective was designing a production-grade, self-service Power BI report that delivers immediate visibility into capital concentration, stage progression, and return drivers.

Dimensional Modeling & Data Architecture

To model multi-stage venture capital investments in Business Intelligence, I generated a synthetic 50,000-row transactional dataset using a deterministic Python script, structuring a clean star schema in Power BI:

  • Fact Table (Fact_Deals): 50,000 investment transaction rows capturing deal dates, investment amounts, equity percentages, round valuations, and exit returns.
  • Dimension Tables: 4 distinct dimension tables providing slicing capability:
    • Dim_Company: Company identifiers, founding year, geography, and operational status.
    • Dim_Sector: Industry categorization (Fintech, SaaS, Healthtech, Climate, E-commerce).
    • Dim_Stage: Investment stage classifications from Pre-Seed to Pre-IPO.
    • Dim_Calendar: Contiguous date table supporting time-intelligence calculations.

Core DAX Measures & Business Logic

Authored 7 fundamental DAX measures enforcing defensive division guards and time-intelligence logic:

  • Total Invested Capital: SUM(Fact_Deals[InvestmentAmount])
  • Average Deal Size: AVERAGE(Fact_Deals[InvestmentAmount])
  • Exit Valuation Multiple (MOIC): DIVIDE(SUM(Fact_Deals[ExitValue]), SUM(Fact_Deals[InvestmentAmount]), 0)
  • Total Closed Deals: DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Deals[DealID])
  • Year-over-Year Investment Growth: VAR PriorYear = CALCULATE([Total Invested Capital], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Calendar[Date])) RETURN DIVIDE([Total Invested Capital] - PriorYear, PriorYear, 0)

Dashboard Layout & AI Diagnostics

The completed report spans 3 dedicated pages containing 8 visual charts and 4 executive KPI summary cards:

  • Page 1: Portfolio Overview: Macro trends in capital deployed, cumulative deals, and vintage performance.
  • Page 2: Sector & Stage Breakdown: Allocation matrix visualizing capital concentration and median round valuations across sectors.
  • Page 3: Returns & Exit Drivers: Exit multiple distributions and vintage cohort analysis.
  • AI Diagnostics Visuals: Integrated Power BI Key Influencers and Decomposition Tree visuals to allow investment partners to interactively explore what factors drive top-quartile exit multiples.

Key Business Intelligence Insights

  • Star schemas accelerate DAX: Designing clean one-to-many relationships without bi-directional cross-filtering keeps report query times sub-second even over large fact tables.
  • Defensive DAX prevents errors: Using DIVIDE() with explicit zero fallbacks prevents divide-by-zero errors in ratio cards.
  • Transparent dataset labeling: Clearly labeling synthetic data in analytics portfolios demonstrates dimensional modeling competence while preserving professional credibility.
